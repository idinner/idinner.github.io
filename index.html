About

I build data products, machine learning systems, and the infrastructure that brings them into production. My work spans recommendation and ranking systems, personalization, experimentation, and causal inference, with a focus on solving growth problems. I combine hands-on modeling and engineering with technical leadership, taking ideas from early development through deployment at scale.

I’m VP of Applied AI/ML at Vivid Seats, where I’ve built and deployed in-house recommendation systems for the app and CRM, increasing conversion rates and expanding gross revenue. I’ve also expanded capabilities in algorithmic performance marketing, pricing, experimentation, and ML architecture.

Previously, I led analytics for growth and brand marketing at Indeed and worked in growth data science at Airbnb. I also advise startups as an operating partner with Builders VC.

Earlier in my career, I served on the business faculty at UNC-Chapel Hill and IE Business School in Madrid. My research has appeared in Harvard Business Review, Journal of Marketing Research, Marketing Science, International Journal of Research in Marketing, Journal of International Business Studies, and Journal of Experimental Psychology: Applied.

I hold a PhD in marketing and an MA in statistics from Columbia University and an SB in mathematics from MIT.
